Three ways to sit

Pick the spot that suits the evening: private, open-air, or a little apart.

Private cabins

Wooden cabins with white curtains and warm LED trim. Draw the curtain and the table is yours for the evening.

Under the flags

Outdoor seating beneath strings of colourful flags and fairy lights, with the painted track right beside you.

The egg chair corner

A hanging rattan chair with red cushions, set in a quiet corner by the net fence. Good for two, better for one with a drink.
